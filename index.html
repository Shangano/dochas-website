Welcome to Dòchas

Home care with heart, dignity and hope.

Dòchas Home Care supports people to live safely, independently and with confidence in the place they know best: home. Our care begins with the person, not a list of tasks. We listen, understand what matters, and provide support with warmth, respect and compassion.

Why Dòchas?

Our name means hope.

Dòchas is a Scottish Gaelic word meaning hope. We chose it because care should never be only about getting through the day. It should help people hold on to who they are, stay connected to the life they know, and look forward with confidence. That sense of hope sits at the heart of Dòchas.
