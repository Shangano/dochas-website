The Dòchas Way

Warm care and professional care aren't opposites. At Dòchas, they belong together.

Dignity first

Every visit protects privacy, explains what's happening, and treats a person's home and belongings with respect.

Independence

We support people to keep doing what they can for themselves, we walk alongside, not take over.

Safety & accountability

Good intentions aren't enough, care follows risk assessments, safeguarding practice, and clear records.

What Dòchas means

Hope. Possibility. Looking forward.

For many people, care arrives at a moment when life has changed, through age, illness, reduced mobility, or a hospital discharge. We don't believe receiving care should mean life suddenly becomes smaller.

Support should help people keep living, staying connected to routines, choices, relationships and community. That sense of hope is at the heart of the name Dòchas.
