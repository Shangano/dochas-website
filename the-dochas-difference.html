About Us

The Dòchas Difference

There are many organisations that provide care. What we want people to remember about Dòchas is how we approach it. We don't believe people should disappear behind care plans, rotas or diagnoses.

Every home has its own rhythm. Every family is different. Every visit is an opportunity to make someone's day safer, easier or simply a little better. As we grow, we stay ambitious about quality without losing the values that led to Dòchas being created in the first place.

What you can expect from Dòchas+

To listen to you, treat you with dignity, respect your home, protect your privacy, involve you in decisions, encourage your independence, explain what we're doing, provide care safely, take concerns seriously, communicate honestly, respect your choices, and recognise when your needs change.

"Every visit belongs to the client."That is the Dòchas Way

See the Dòchas Way in practice

Whether you're looking for care, or thinking about joining our team, this is the culture behind every visit.
