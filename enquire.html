Looking for care?

Looking for care can feel overwhelming, especially if it's the first time you've arranged support for yourself or someone close to you. You don't need everything figured out first, an enquiry is simply the beginning of a conversation.

You might be:

  • looking for care for yourself
  • helping a parent or relative
  • planning for a hospital discharge
  • looking for extra support as needs have changed
  • a social worker or care manager making a referral
  • simply trying to understand what's available
This form doesn't ask for detailed medical information. Anything more sensitive is collected securely at the right stage, once we've spoken.

Professional referral?

We welcome enquiries from social workers, care managers, healthcare professionals and local authority teams. A separate, secure referral process is available where more detail is needed.
