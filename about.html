About Dòchas

Who we are

Dòchas Home Care is built around a simple belief: good care should never feel rushed, impersonal, or reduced to a list of tasks. We support adults in their own homes, helping them stay safe, independent, and connected to the life they know.

Every person we support has their own routines, preferences, history and way of doing things. Our role isn't to take over, it's to work alongside them, and help them keep as much choice, independence and dignity as possible. That's the foundation of Dòchas.

Our story

Built from years of real experience in care

Dòchas was founded by Annah and Gail, who between them spent years working inside the care sector. They saw the very best of care, and they saw what could be lost when services became too focused on schedules, tasks and paperwork.

A shower could become a task. Preparing lunch could become a task. Even conversation could become something squeezed into whatever time was left. But for the person receiving support, these aren't tasks, this is their life.

Annah and Gail wanted to build a service that remembered that: one where dignity, kindness, safety, professionalism and genuine human connection could all exist together.

"A service where entering someone's home is treated as a privilege, not a routine stop on a rota."Why Dòchas was created

Our mission

To provide safe, compassionate and person-centred care that enables people to live at home with dignity, independence and confidence, understanding each person as an individual, and supporting the people who provide that care to feel proud of the standards they represent.

Our vision

To help shape a better standard of home care, professional without becoming impersonal, compassionate without losing boundaries, safe without unnecessarily taking control away. As Dòchas grows, we stay true to why it was created: people first.

The Dòchas Way

Every visit belongs to the client

Our culture, not a single policy, brought together across everyday care.

Person-centred

No two people are the same, so care is never delivered as though they are. We take time to understand routines, preferences, communication style, and what outcomes matter most.

Dignity

Speaking respectfully, explaining what we're doing before we do it, protecting privacy, and never forgetting that needing help doesn't make someone less entitled to choice or control.

Independence

Support is offered only where it's needed, walking alongside rather than taking over, wherever it's safe and appropriate.

Choice & control

We listen, explain, and respect informed decisions, including where a person's choice involves a level of risk we help them understand.

Compassion

Paying attention. Noticing when something has changed. Understanding that a "routine" task can feel deeply personal to the person receiving it.

Safety

Following care plans, risk assessments and moving & handling guidance, and encouraging staff to stop and ask if something doesn't feel right.

Professionalism

Confidentiality, honesty, reliable attendance, accurate records, and speaking up when something is wrong, because care happens inside people's private homes.
