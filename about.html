Equality, diversity and inclusion

Every person treated fairly and with respect

This applies to clients, employees, families, applicants and everyone working with Dòchas. We don't believe good care can exist where discrimination or prejudice is tolerated, and equality doesn't always mean treating everyone identically, sometimes people need different forms of support to achieve fair outcomes.
