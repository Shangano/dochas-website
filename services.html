Our services

Care that fits around the person

Care shouldn't begin with a standard package, it should begin with a conversation. We take time to understand what support is needed, what someone can and wants to keep doing independently, and how care can fit into their existing life.

Services depend on each person's assessed needs and what Dòchas is registered and able to provide.

Who we support

People at every stage of needing a little more support

Older adults

Support to remain safe, independent and connected at home, at whatever level suits changing needs.

Adults with disabilities

Person-centred support that respects independence, choice, and how someone wants to live their life.

People recovering from illness or surgery

Short or longer-term support to recover safely at home, including after a hospital stay.

People living with dementia

Patient, familiar, reassuring support that follows routines and reduces confusion or distress.

Families & unpaid carers

Respite support that gives family carers a break, with confidence their relative is properly looked after.

Anyone finding daily life harder to manage

If day-to-day tasks are becoming difficult for any reason, we're happy to talk it through, whatever the cause.

What we offer

Our services

Personal Care

Washing, showering, bathing, dressing, grooming, oral care and toileting, delivered sensitively, protecting privacy and dignity at every stage, and encouraging independence wherever it's safe to do so.

Support With Daily Living

Help with the ordinary things that make the biggest difference to remaining at home, never about taking control, always about keeping everyday life manageable.

Meal Preparation & Nutrition

Preparing meals and drinks, encouraging good food and fluid intake, and noticing, then recording and reporting, changes like reduced appetite.

Medication Support

Trained staff provide the appropriate level of assistance, in line with our medication procedures and each person's care plan. Treated as a serious responsibility.

Mobility & Moving Safely

Supporting safe movement around the home, following assessed moving & handling guidance, while helping people stay as independent as possible.

Companionship & Social Support

Conversation, activities, and staying connected to the people and things that matter, warm and interested, while remaining professional. The time belongs to the client.

Community Support

Support to access the community, attend appointments or activities, and stay connected, active and involved, home care doesn't have to mean staying inside.

Support Following Hospital Discharge

Working with the person, their family, and the professionals involved to support a safe, needs-based transition back home.

Respite for Families

Where appropriate, giving family members and unpaid carers time away from caring responsibilities, knowing their relative is properly supported.

Professional support

Working alongside social workers, discharge teams & healthcare professionals

We regularly work alongside the professionals supporting a person's wider care, and we understand what a smooth, well-communicated referral needs to look like.

  • Clear, timely communication throughout the referral and assessment process
  • Support with hospital discharge planning, on the timescales that discharge requires
  • Willingness to attend case conferences, reviews or multi-disciplinary meetings where appropriate
  • Accurate, up-to-date records and reporting in line with Care Inspectorate requirements
  • A point of contact who understands regulated care, not just a booking line

Making a referral

If you're a social worker, care manager, discharge coordinator or healthcare professional looking to arrange care for someone, we'd welcome hearing from you. A secure referral route is available where more detail is needed than a general enquiry form allows.

Make a professional referral

Not sure what type of care you need?

You don't need to know the name of a service before contacting us. Tell us what's happening, and what you or your family member are finding difficult, we'll talk it through with you.
