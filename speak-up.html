We want you to feel able to speak up

That applies to people who use our service, their families, our staff, and professionals working alongside us. Raising a concern will not, in itself, affect the care you receive.

Emergency or immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 straight away. Web forms and enquiry chat on this site are not monitored as an emergency service.
