Help & feedback

We want you to feel able to speak up

Whether you have a concern, want to make a complaint, need to tell us someone may be at risk, or simply want to recognise something that went well, we want to hear from you.

You will be heard

Raising a concern will not, in itself, affect the care you receive.

Emergency or immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 straight away. Web forms and enquiry chat on this site are not monitored as an emergency service.
