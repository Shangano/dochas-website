Complaints, concerns & feedback

We want you to feel able to speak up

That applies to people who use our service, their families, our staff, and professionals working alongside us. Raising a concern will not, in itself, affect the care you receive or the way you're treated by Dòchas.

Emergency or immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, please contact the emergency services first, don't wait for a web form response.

I have a concern

Something is worrying you, but it isn't necessarily a formal complaint, poor communication, visit timing, or a change in how care is provided.

Raise a concern →

I want to make a complaint

Quality of care, staff conduct, missed visits, dignity and respect, or a previous complaint that wasn't resolved.

Make a complaint →

I believe someone may be at risk

Concerns about abuse, neglect, exploitation or another form of harm. Handled under our Adult Support and Protection Policy.

Report a safeguarding concern →

I'm a member of staff

Whistleblowing covers wrongdoing or serious matters affecting others, the organisation or the public interest, different from a personal grievance.

Speak up →

I want to stay anonymous

You can raise a concern without giving your name. This may make it harder for us to follow up, verify details or keep you updated.

Report anonymously →

I want to give positive feedback

Tell us when a member of staff has made a real difference, it helps us recognise excellent care.

Send a compliment →

What happens next

After you make a complaint

Acknowledged

We record it and make sure we understand the issue.

Considered

We review the relevant information and speak with people involved where necessary.

Responded to

We explain the outcome and any action to be taken.

Learned from

We identify lessons or improvements where appropriate.

Not satisfied?
