Submit an enquiry or contact formName, phone number, email, the area you're enquiring about, and details of the support you're looking for

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and explain the support we can offer
  • To assess, arrange, and safely deliver care where someone becomes a client
  • To process job applications and carry out safer recruitment checks
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and safeguarding obligations, including those set by the Care Inspectorate
  • To improve this website and understand how it's used

We do not use enquiry or contact information for marketing without your clear consent, and we never sell personal information to third parties.

4. Our legal basis for using it

Under UK GDPR, we rely on one or more of the following, depending on the situation: your consent (for example, submitting a form), performance of a contract (for example, delivering agreed care), a legal obligation (for example, safeguarding or regulatory reporting), or our legitimate interests (for example, responding to a general enquiry). Where health or care-related information is involved, we apply the additional protections required for special category data.

5. Who we share information with

We do not share personal information for marketing purposes. We may share relevant information with:

  • Care Inspectorate, local authorities, or NHS professionals involved in your care or referral
  • Emergency services or safeguarding authorities, where there is a risk to someone's safety
  • Service providers who support our operations (for example, secure IT or payroll systems), under appropriate confidentiality agreements
  • Regulators or legal authorities, where we are required to by law

6. How long we keep it

General website enquiries that don't proceed further are kept only as long as needed to respond, and are then deleted. Client and employment records are kept in line with Care Inspectorate and statutory retention requirements, which are typically longer, reflecting the regulated nature of care records. 9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to: access the information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, ask us to delete information (where applicable), object to or restrict certain uses, and request a copy of information you've provided in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your information has been mishandled.
